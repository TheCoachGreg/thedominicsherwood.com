The Littles

Horror · Paramount

Written and directed by Andrew Duplessie, this stop-motion and live-action horror short stars Violet McGraw alongside Janel Parrish and Dominic. Animated by Anthony Scott (The Nightmare Before Christmas) with puppets from Katy Strutz (Guillermo del Toro’s Pinocchio), it premiered to a sold-out crowd at New York Comic Con, won at the Indy Shorts Film Festival, and is being adapted as a feature at Paramount.

About

Dominic

Dominic Sherwood is an English actor, born in Tunbridge Wells, Kent in 1990. He grew up in Kent, studied drama in Maidstone and Sevenoaks, and worked abroad before committing to acting full time.

His early credits include a young Mick Jagger in Not Fade Away and Christian Ozera in Vampire Academy. In 2015 he appeared in Taylor Swift’s ‘Style’ video, and later that year was cast as Jace Herondale in Freeform’s Shadowhunters, which he led across three seasons. He has since worked across television and film, from Kurt in Penny Dreadful: City of Angels to Jeff Murphy in Netflix’s Partner Track.

He is currently filming the action-thriller White Tide and features in the horror short The Littles. He shares his work on Instagram at @domsherwood.
